The flourishes, by token

  • Stripe under the header: four film-box bands (--stripe-1…4, --stripe-h 0 in Light and Dark).
  • Sprocket strip on dark feature cards: a row of film perforations cut from the page colour.
  • Snapshot card beside the hero: today's top project as an instant print, with a camera date stamp. Decorative; hidden from screen readers and on phones.
  • Track tints: six hue pairs for chips and avatars, cycled by track, every pair at 6:1 or better.

What stays fixed

  • Layout, spacing, radii and copy. A theme never moves or renames anything.
  • Body text stays Inter in every theme; only display and number fonts change.
  • Every text pair passes WCAG AA (4.5:1). Golden Hour's accent on its page colour is the tightest, at 4.86:1.
  • Upvote state reads from fill and border, never from hue alone.
